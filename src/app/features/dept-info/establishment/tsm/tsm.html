Establishment

Tourist Information Centre (TIC)

TTDCL has set up Tourist Information Centre (TIC), at different public places in the Country. These TIC's showcase the information about the potential tourists spots as well as the tourism product of the State for marketing purpose. This is showcased to make information available to the visiting tourists. Besides, the facilities for booking of package tours/ tourist vehicles are made available from these TIC's. Presently, TIC's are being managed by the staffs of the Corporation. TTDCL wants to outsource the operation of TIC's on PPP mode without deviating the existing objectives of these facilities.
