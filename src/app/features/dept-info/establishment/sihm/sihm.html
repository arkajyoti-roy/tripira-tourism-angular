Tripura Tourism, Government of Tripura (GoT), through Tripura Tourism Development Corporation Ltd. (TTDCL), has embarked on a major initiative for growth of tourism and hospitality sector in Tripura. As part of this initiative, GoT has set up a world class Institute of Hotel Management at Agartala. The institute is to be run under Public-Private-Partnership (PPP) Model. Society for State Institute of Hotel Management Catering Service & Applied Nutrition, Anandanagar, Agartala, invites application from interested Firms/ Organizations for running of State Institute of Hotel Management (SIHM) in Private Public Partnership (PPP) Model.
