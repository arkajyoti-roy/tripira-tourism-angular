State Animal

Phayre's Langur (Trachypithecus phayrei)

Indeed, Phayre's Langur (Trachypithecus phayrei) holds the esteemed title of being the state animal of Tripura, adding to the region's unique biodiversity and natural heritage.

State Bird

Green Imperial Pigeon (Ducula aenea)

The Green Imperial Pigeon (Ducula aenea) proudly serves as the state bird of Tripura, symbolizing the avian diversity and splendor of the state's natural environment.

State Tree

Agar (Aquilaria malaccensis)

Agar which is a native of Tripura is an evergreen tree. It has an embedded historical and cultural significance with the state's culture and environment.

State Flower

Nageswar (Mesua ferrea)

The Nageswar (Mesua ferrea) holds the prestigious title of the state flower of Tripura, adding its unique beauty and presence within the state's botanical landscape.

State Fruit

Queen Pineapple (Ananas comosus)

The Queen Pineapple (Ananas comosus) is the state fruit of Tripura, signifying the agricultural significance of this delicious and popular fruit in the state's agricultural and culinary traditions.
