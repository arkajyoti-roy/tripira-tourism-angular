Hill Ranges

Tripura is a land of high hills, hillocks and valleys. Five major hill ranges traverse the state from North-West to South-East direction. These are Boromura, Atharamura, Longtharai, Shakhan and Jampui Hills. The highest peak of the state is Betling Shib (939 meters) situated in the Jampui Hill range. The topography is undulating and the terrain is mostly hilly. The state is completely landlocked, bounded by the international border with Bangladesh on three sides.

Climate

The climate of Tripura is typically tropical monsoon type. The state experiences a warm and humid summer followed by a moderate winter. The main seasons are Summer (March to May), Monsoon (June to September), Autumn (October to November) and Winter (December to February). Summer temperatures range from 24°C to 36°C, while winter temperatures range from 13°C to 27°C. The highest temperature is generally recorded in the month of May and the lowest in the month of January.

The state receives an average annual rainfall of about 2500 mm, mostly concentrated during the monsoon season. The high humidity and rainfall contribute to the rich flora and fauna of the state.

Rainfall

Tripura receives heavy rainfall from the south-west monsoons. The rainy season generally starts from June and continues up to September. The state receives an average rainfall of over 2000 mm.

The average annual rainfall in the state is 2500 mm. The maximum rainfall is received during the monsoon period from June to September. The rainfall is generally higher in the northern and eastern parts of the state compared to the western and southern parts.

Major Rivers in Tripura

A number of rivers flow through the state of Tripura. The major rivers are Gomati, Khowai, Manu, Haora, Muhuri, Feni, and Juri. All these rivers are rain-fed and ephemeral in nature. They flow into Bangladesh. The Gomati is the largest river in the state.

Population

Tripura is the second most populous state in the North Eastern Region after Assam. According to the 2011 census, the total population of the state is 3,673,917. The density of population is 350 persons per sq. km.

The state has a diverse population comprising various ethnic groups. The tribal population constitutes about 31.8% of the total population. There are 19 notified Scheduled Tribes in the state. The literacy rate of Tripura is 87.22%, which is significantly higher than the national average.

Tripura comprises mostly of tribal and Bengali population. The Bengalis constitute the majority of the population. The state has a rich cultural heritage and is known for its exquisite handicrafts, handlooms, music, and dance. The indigenous tribes have their own distinct culture, traditions, and language, which adds to the diversity of the state.

The state government has taken several initiatives to improve the living standards of the people by providing better healthcare, education, and employment opportunities. The focus is on sustainable development and inclusive growth, ensuring that the benefits reach every corner of the state.
