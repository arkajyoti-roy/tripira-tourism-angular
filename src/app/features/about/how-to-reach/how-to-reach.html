Fly to Agartala

By Air

"

Agartala Airport (Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport) is the second busiest airport in North East India after Guwahati. It is well connected to major cities in India like Kolkata, Guwahati, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Mumbai, etc. Several airlines operate regular flights to and from Agartala, providing convenient air travel options for domestic and international passengers.

Scenic Highways

By Road

"

Tripura is connected by road with Guwahati via Shillong by National Highway No. 8. It takes about 24 hours by road from Guwahati to reach Agartala. Good quality AC and Non-AC night bus services are available from Guwahati, Silchar, and other major cities of the North Eastern region.

The NH-8 connects Agartala to all the district headquarters within the state, making road transport a viable option for traveling across Tripura.

Railways

By Train

"

Agartala is well connected by railway network. The railway station at Agartala is one of the best in North East India. Passengers can easily travel to Agartala by train from major cities like Delhi, Kolkata, Guwahati, Silchar, Lumding, etc. The train journey offers a scenic view of the lush green valleys and hills of the region.

* Some trains might be temporarily not operating or running on special schedules. Please check the official IRCTC website for the latest train schedules and availability.

Cross-Border Routes

By International Bus

"

Agartala, the capital of Tripura, is connected by road with Bangladesh. There is a direct bus service running between Agartala and Dhaka (capital of Bangladesh) operated by the TRTC. This service offers a convenient and affordable travel option for international passengers crossing the border.

The journey from Agartala to Dhaka takes approximately 4-5 hours depending on border clearance.

For more details, contact the TRTC Office at Krishnanagar, Agartala.
